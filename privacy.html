Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 · Planet Tweaks (planettweaks.website)

1. Information We Collect

Planet Tweaks is a local Windows optimization utility. You can use the application in Offline Guest Mode without creating an account. If you choose to sign in with Email or Google OAuth to sync your profile, we receive only the basic account identifiers you authorize:

  • Your email address and unique authentication user ID (provided by Supabase Auth / Google OAuth openid, email, and profile scopes).
  • Your chosen GamerTag, avatar selection, and application version metadata stored in your cloud profile.

2. Local Hardware Telemetry & Settings

All Windows tweaks, keyboard remappings, backup snapshots, and live hardware telemetry (CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, and network stats) are processed strictly locally on your PC. Planet Tweaks does not upload hardware sensor data, keystrokes, or personal files to external servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

Account information is used solely to authenticate your cloud session and sync your Planet Tweaks GamerTag and preferences across devices. We never sell, rent, or share user data with third-party advertisers.

4. Security & Data Retention

Desktop session tokens are encrypted locally using Windows DPAPI. Cloud authentication and profile records are protected by Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) so each account can access only its own profile. You may sign out at any time or request deletion of your cloud profile by contacting smurkishmode22@gmail.com.

5. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email smurkishmode22@gmail.com.
